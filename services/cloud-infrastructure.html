Frequently asked questions

Is the cloud safe for business data?

AWS and Google Cloud offer stronger security than nearly any on-premise setup — encryption, redundancy, and compliance certifications — when configured correctly, which is our job.

How long does cloud migration take?

A typical small-business migration completes in 1–3 weeks with zero or near-zero downtime, planned around your business hours.
