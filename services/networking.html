Frequently asked questions

Why is our office Wi-Fi so slow?

Usually: consumer-grade equipment, poor access-point placement, channel interference, or an undersized internet plan. We diagnose and fix all four.

Do we need a VPN for remote workers?

If staff access business systems from home or travel, yes — a VPN encrypts that traffic and keeps company data off public networks.
